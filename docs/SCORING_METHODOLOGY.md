# Scoring Methodology

Operational screening only. Missed = 30 points; delayed = 25; due = 12; days past supplied due date = up to 20; no follow-up contact = 10; local high-priority flag = 10; pending reminder = 5. Cap = 100.

0–24 Routine; 25–49 Attention; 50–74 High Priority; 75–100 Urgent Review.

These classes do not establish medical urgency or vaccine eligibility.
